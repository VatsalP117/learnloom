# Learnloom paid-only pricing recommendation

Date: 2026-08-16

## Decision

| Plan | Monthly price | Learning streams | Generated lessons |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Essential | $9 | 3 total | Unlimited |
| Pro | $19 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

There is no permanent free plan. The packaging metric is the number of learning
streams a learner can organize, not lesson-generation volume. Both paid plans
may retain non-marketing operational safeguards for abuse, reliability, and
unexpected cost spikes; these must not be presented as a monthly lesson quota.

## Product rationale

- A stream is a customer-legible value unit: it corresponds to a sustained
  learning goal. A generation credit is an implementation cost and makes the
  learner ration the core habit the product is meant to build.
- Three streams supports a focused individual while making the Pro upgrade
  easy to understand for broad, parallel interests.
- $9 sits in the entry subscription corridor established by learning and
  knowledge tools. $19 is a familiar premium-AI anchor and gives enough room
  for higher usage without introducing generation anxiety.
- Paid-only packaging avoids a weak free experience and keeps qualification,
  support, and model spend concentrated on customers with intent.

## Market anchors reviewed

- [Quizlet plans](https://quizlet.com/upgrade) anchor consumer study software at
  a low annualized monthly price.
- [Mindgrasp plans](https://app.mindgrasp.ai/pick-plan) span roughly $10-$15 on
  monthly billing for AI study features.
- [Readwise Reader pricing](https://readwise.io/pricing/reader) anchors a focused
  knowledge workflow around $13 monthly.
- [Google One AI plans](https://one.google.com/about/plans) use familiar $10 and
  $20 premium-AI price points.

These are positioning anchors, not feature-equivalent competitors. The proposed
$9/$19 ladder deliberately uses a familiar buying range while differentiating
tiers on durable learning organization.

## Launch measurements

Review after the first 30-50 qualified checkout exposures, without changing
both price and packaging at once:

- checkout start and paid conversion by selected plan;
- Essential-to-Pro selection and later upgrade intent;
- streams created per paid account and the share reaching the third stream;
- lessons generated, model cost, and gross margin by plan;
- 30-day retention, cancellation reasons, payment failures, and refunds.

Trigger an operational review if unlimited generation materially breaks the
target contribution margin. First optimize generation cost and abuse controls;
only introduce a visible usage restriction if evidence shows those measures are
insufficient.
