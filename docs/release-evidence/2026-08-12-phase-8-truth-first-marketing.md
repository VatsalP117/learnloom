# Phase 8 truth-first marketing — repository evidence, 2026-08-12

## Implemented and verified

- The hero identifies the launch hypothesis—AI and software professionals in
  fast-moving fields—and leads with the costly workflow: repeatedly rebuilding
  context from feeds, bookmarks, and one-off chats.
- The promise is explicit: give Learnloom a topic; it finds and ranks useful
  sources, teaches the next concept, and revisits it before it fades.
- The homepage now demonstrates the complete loop in one coherent example:
  topic and outcome → explainable source portfolio → approximately 12-minute
  lesson → answer-before-reveal retrieval → an adapted next lesson chosen from
  gaps, prior concepts, recall, and available time.
- Four homepage links expose the complete AI-evaluation Dossier before signup.
- The comparison section describes each workflow's strength and tradeoff for
  general chat assistants, NotebookLM, Readwise/read-later, Recall/knowledge
  maps, and Learnloom. Copy is explicitly scoped to workflow defaults rather
  than unverifiable universal limitations.
- Source methodology, frozen claim evidence, private-by-default behavior,
  correction/reporting, privacy, and model limitations now sit directly on the
  main conversion path.
- The former publishing-led section and fictitious activity cards were removed
  from the primary proof sequence. The remaining walkthrough identifies itself
  as an illustration of product behavior and contains no fabricated users,
  activity, progress, or performance numbers. Optional publishing appears only
  after the private learning value proposition.
- Homepage title, description, Open Graph, and Twitter decoration share the
  topic-to-learning-path and stay-current positioning.
