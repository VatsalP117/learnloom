# Launch video sound effects

The V1 interaction layer uses selected recordings from
[Keyboard Soundpack #1](https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes)
by unicae_games, released under CC0.

The source pack was recorded from a Cherry KC 1000 keyboard with a Shure SM7B.
Only its softest human typing performances and restrained single-key recordings
are included here. `scripts/generate-launch-sfx.mjs` trims, filters, places, and
mixes those recordings into `public/launch-sfx-v1.m4a`.
